tag: "Conference"
blocks:
  - type: "p"
    text: "In recognition of our 25th annual meeting at [Bellairs](https://www.mcgill.ca/bellairs/), this year's [Systems Biology workshop](https://mikehallett.science/#barbados) (Holetown, Barbados, February 5–12, 2027) asks how biological systems change over time. Whether reconstructing protein histories, tracking tumor evolution, watching ecosystems respond to pressure, studying regulatory network dynamics, or building adaptive computational models, we are all asking variations of the same core questions: How do biological systems generate novelty? What constrains transformation? How do we predict future states from present patterns?"
  - type: "p"
    text: "As is tradition, 40–50 researchers will share their core research programs, drawn from structural biology and molecular evolution, cancer biology and somatic evolution, microbial ecology and environmental science, computational biology and systems modeling, and developmental biology and regulatory networks. Each invitee dedicates a fraction of their talk to perspective on dynamic change in their own system — through deep evolutionary time, real-time dynamics, or conceptual shifts in understanding."
