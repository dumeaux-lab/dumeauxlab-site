title: "New preprint — Murmurent, an agentic AI environment for collaborative biomedical research"
excerpt: "Murmurent is open-source infrastructure that sits beneath agentic AI and gives research labs shared projects, specialized agents, memory, traceability and data-governance safeguards."
tag: "Research"
blocks:
  - type: "p"
    text: "Our new preprint with the Hallett lab is out on [bioRxiv](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.24.753565): *An agentic AI environment to support biomedical research in collaborative academic environments* (Wu et al. 2026)."
  - type: "p"
    text: "Agentic AI has made it much easier to bring computational and statistical approaches into day-to-day research, but labs still have to work out how to use it, set procedures for managing and sharing data, and protect privacy. [Murmurent](/software/murmurent/) is shared software that sits beneath the AI and provides these skills, procedures and protections, so each lab doesn't have to build them on its own."
  - type: "ul"
    items:
      - "Shared projects and *choreographies* involving multiple group members"
      - "Specialized agents for software and statistical development, data visualization, literature search, EDID review and more"
      - "Tiered memory and traceability records of research decisions"
      - "Enforced data-governance procedures across all lab members"
      - "Multi-user collaboration within and across labs"
  - type: "p"
    text: "As a case study, we use the system to search for inhibitors of Pin1, a protein whose shallow catalytic site makes it difficult to target."
  - type: "p"
    text: "Murmurent is open source: [code on GitHub](https://github.com/hallettmiket/murmurent) · [7-minute getting-started video](https://mikehallett.science/assets/videos/murmurent_getting_started.html) · [documentation](https://mikehallett.science/murmurent/)."
  - type: "p"
    text: "Congratulations to first author Timothy Wu and to all co-authors, including lab members **Mohamed Meawad**, **Yubing Xia**, **Bushra Kabbani**, **Eliseos J. Mucaki** and alumna **Athena Ma**."
